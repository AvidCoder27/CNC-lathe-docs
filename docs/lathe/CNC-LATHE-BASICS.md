# Our Lathe

Our lathe is pretty basic. It has a relatively small radius of operation around the part meaning you cannot make anything much larger than a couple inches in diameter.

It has only one spindle which is controlled manually with a knob. Thus, we cannot run any threading operations on it because the speed (RPM) of the spindle cannot be controlled with enough accuracy.
